Nobody here gets a better view than you.

Six dorm rooms, twenty-six beds, and a second building with the private rooms in it. Every dorm bed costs the same LKR 7,500 a night — the four-bed rooms, the six-bed room, no difference.

So there is no upgrade on the part that matters. Nobody staying tonight has a better claim on the roof, the long table or the view over the valley than you do. The bed is the ticket. The roof is the reason to come.

Breakfast is at eight, upstairs, under a green translucent roof that turns the whole terrace and everyone sitting in it a slightly unreal shade of sea-glass. A few steps away, past the railing, the light snaps back to normal and there is a mountain ridge across the gap. That is the building. That is what LKR 7,500 buys.

The roof is the whole argument

The top floor has no wall on the valley side. Just a railing, and then the drop, and then the ridge.

It is roofed in translucent green panels on a black steel frame, so the light up there is green and the shade is cool. Long timber tables with benches — no two-tops, nobody gets a private corner. Rattan lampshades on rope. Hanging egg chairs. A dog called Sando. Next to it, under its own roof, a pool table.

Breakfast is served here between 8:00 and 11:00, and it is included with every bed. Drinking water is free, from a filtered dispenser, all day.

Getting here

We are on the hillside above Ella. The walk into the centre of town takes under ten minutes — down on the way in, up on the way home.

That climb is the whole point. Five minutes above a town centre is a quieter place than a town centre, and the higher you go the more of the valley you get back. The last of it is a concrete drive between trees, and then the green roof.

Arriving late, or with a pack you would rather not carry up a hill? Message us before you set off and we will tell you exactly where to turn. Or take a tuk-tuk — the drivers know the road.

About these photographs

Every photograph of the hostel on this site was taken here, on the property, by someone standing in the room you are looking at. Nothing is stock. Nothing is a render. Nothing was shot at a different hostel and captioned as ours.
